# PeakMotion SEO Keyword Ownership

This file is the fixed source of truth for commercial-page keyword ownership. Each primary phrase belongs to one page only. Other pages may use the phrase as a contextual internal-link anchor, but must not use it as their own SEO title, H1, schema `name`, or repeated page-wide target.

| Page | Route | Primary keyword | Secondary scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | `/` | `custom sports socks manufacturer` | `custom socks manufacturer` |
| Private Label | `/private-label-sports-socks` | `private label socks manufacturer` | Brand launch, packaging and retail collections |
| Manufacturing | `/socks-manufacturing` | `OEM socks manufacturer` | Production process, quality control and packaging |
| Custom Grip | `/custom-grip-socks` | `custom grip socks manufacturer` | Wholesale grip socks and custom grip programs |
| Yoga / Pilates | `/yoga-pilates-grip-socks-supplier` | `pilates grip socks manufacturer` | Yoga, barre, studio and wellness programs |
| Football | `/football-socks-manufacturer` | `football socks manufacturer` | Soccer terminology and club/team long tails |
| Running | `/custom-running-socks-manufacturer` | `running socks manufacturer` | Running clubs, marathon events and private label running socks |
| Basketball | `/custom-basketball-training-socks` | `basketball socks manufacturer` | Team, training and private label basketball socks |
| Future Trampoline | Reserved | `trampoline grip socks manufacturer` | Do not assign until a dedicated page is launched |
| Products | `/products` | `custom sports socks` | `OEM sock styles`; never target a manufacturer phrase |

## Enforcement rules

1. The assigned primary phrase may appear in the owner page's SEO title, H1, opening paragraph and WebPage schema.
2. Product detail pages use product names and product intent, not `manufacturer` phrases.
3. Blog posts use informational long-tail intent such as “how to choose” and link to the owner commercial page.
4. Global header and footer copy stays neutral and does not repeat a commercial page's primary keyword across the entire site.
5. New commercial pages must be checked against this table before publication.
